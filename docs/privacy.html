Privacy

Tor Event Calendar AI is a local client for your private calendar. Tokens are stored in your OS credential store; non-secret profile identity metadata is kept in a user-scoped config. No token is placed in a prompt, URL, public package or browser localStorage. The client sends authenticated calendar requests only to the pinned service origin and refuses redirects.

The service stores verified account identity, your own events/files, hashed token metadata and short-lived pairing requests. Publication receipts and pending receipt journals can contain external post IDs/URLs. Other calendar accounts cannot access your resources. The client adds no analytics; the host AI product and service infrastructure have their own policies. Your AI host receives the calendar content you explicitly request through it.

You can revoke connections in calendar settings or with the CLI. Removing a skill/CLI preserves local connection metadata until explicitly disconnected. Social network credentials are not bundled or shared. The client has no universal social publisher or cloud MCP connector.

Contact and privacy requests: project support. Do not put private events, tokens or files in public issues.
